# AFA-LAUNCH — Level 1: The Silent Transfer

## Delivered experience
Full-screen Red/Blue team lobby, supervised-training acknowledgement, capacity and readiness checks, owner/admin launch countdown, synchronized bank-contract stage, two-page versioned contract, separate round bank with 50,000 DEMO ALL allocation, Demo IBAN verification and exact-value transfers, owner-only incident preview/release, live student briefing, searchable forensic records, private team evidence pins and notebook, versioned team reports, owner report freeze and rubric/verdict, shared debrief and locked Level 2 checkpoint. Ordinary bank and previous Cyber Arena remain available and independent. Level 2 gameplay is not implemented.

## Important rules
Only approved active customer accounts can join student teams. Owner/admin can create/start rounds and open banking; only owner releases the incident, freezes reports, publishes the verdict or cancels a round. Finance gets no game-director permission by default. One live round is supported at a time. A student chooses Red/Blue; this never changes banking roles. Roster locks at launch. At least one ready student per team is required. The owner sees connected counts and decides when to start; disconnected ready students can reconnect and recover stage from D1. Every enrolled active customer must accept the round contract before banking opens.

The round contract records its own version and acceptance timestamp, and explicitly accepting it also records the currently displayed bank-terms version. Lobby access is allowed before bank contract acceptance. Initial password-change requirements and active-account checks still apply. Existing non-game bank terms are retained for ordinary bank access.

## Money and incident integrity
All amounts are integer hundredths of DEMO ALL. Joining creates a round wallet with a single 50,000 ALL allocation; repeat join cannot reset or increase it. A round-only beneficiary named AnOnYmOuS-RuSsIa has no users-table identity, email or login. It is excluded from student teams. Ordinary users.balance and the ordinary transactions ledger are untouched.

The owner action inserts one incident row per round. Database triggers validate owner role, banking stage, eligible students and balances. If any eligible participant has under 10,000 ALL, the entire event fails with no debit. Each active enrolled customer who accepted the contract is debited exactly 10,000 ALL and the total is credited atomically to the beneficiary. Owner, administrator, finance, unenrolled, inactive accounts and beneficiary are excluded. Unique incident and transaction keys prevent duplicate/replayed releases. The incident, generated evidence and round-stage transition commit in one D1 batch. Students may make ordinary round transfers before/during investigation; review/completion freezes transfers.

## A case with a provable answer
The fictional legacy-transfer-service accepted CRED-017 after password rotation EVT-219 because existing service credentials were not revoked and the supplied validation excerpt did not compare credential generations. BATCH-041 links that credential to the virtual ledger entries. Students can correlate service issuance, password-change, batch request, prepared code and actual round transfer records; background records provide filtering practice. Authentication/code artifacts are explicitly fabricated. The real bank is not weakened and does not expose passwords or usable tokens. The answer key stays in server code, is returned only to the owner after release, and the explanatory debrief is returned to students only on completion. No answer key is embedded in the frontend bundle.

## Team work and assessment
Pins/notes are selected by authenticated participant and server-derived team. Clients cannot choose another team's identity. Private notes and opponent reports are not returned to students. Owner/admin can see submitted team reports; owner answer key is more restrictive. Reports require at least three distinct existing evidence references, named beneficiary/service/credential, root cause, timeline and remediation. Immutable report revisions carry server timestamps; the latest revision is assessed. Per-request keys prevent report replay. Team evidence and reports freeze for review with database guards against racing writes.

Rubric: root cause 35, evidence 30, timeline 20, remediation 10. Timing bonus is calculated by server from report timestamps only when root>=25 and evidence>=20: 5 for first/tied valid report, 3 for a later valid report, 0 without a valid report. Owner supplies reasoning and confirms an override if winner differs from totals. No points for an unsubmitted report. Verdict is single-write and audited; completion moves students to debrief. Cancellation preserves history and creates no winner.

## Realtime / reconnect
LaunchHub is a SQLite-backed Cloudflare Durable Object with hibernating WebSockets. It broadcasts refresh signals only; all private state is fetched through existing authenticated Worker routes. Origin is checked at upgrade; server supplies user identity to the private namespace. Presence deduplicates users across sockets. Client reconnects after disconnect, refreshes on focus, polls every 30 seconds while connected and every 5 seconds while disconnected. No client WebSocket message can mutate game state. Bank polling pauses inside the game. WebSockets and state refresh were checked with separate student/owner sessions.

Owner releases the incident only after contracts and banking stage are complete. No production exercise is started automatically.

## Boundaries for later phases
Single cohort/live round, Red and Blue roles only for Level 1. Existing Arena keeps SOC/Auditor separately. No real exploit execution, file uploads, voice chat, external logs, external financial integrations or automatic Level 2. Authentication events in the case are prepared evidence, not a real breach. Notes/history are bounded for this cohort (80 notebook entries, 100 wallet transactions, 20 round summaries); multi-cohort/pagination/export are future work. Reports are manual academic assessment with a common rubric, not an NLP grader. Mobile uses stacked panels and swipeable case navigation. Workers/D1/Durable Objects free quotas still apply to actual classroom traffic; no paid plan is enabled by code.
